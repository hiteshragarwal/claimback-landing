Frequently asked questions

Everything you need
to know.

Is this legal advice?
No. ClaimBack provides AI-generated informational analysis to help you understand your options. We are not lawyers and do not provide legal advice. Always verify citations independently before acting.
What if my claim is genuinely not covered?
We analyse every case honestly. If your rejection is valid and the policy clearly supports it, our AI will tell you that — with a low win score and an explanation. We never inflate scores to take your ₹999.
Is my data safe?
Your documents are encrypted at rest and in transit. We use Supabase (hosted on AWS Mumbai). Documents are processed by Anthropic's Claude AI — disclosed in our Privacy Policy. We never share data with insurers.
What types of health insurance do you cover?
All health insurance: individual, family floater, and group/corporate policies. All IRDAI-registered insurers. We do not currently cover life insurance or motor insurance claims.
What if the insurer still rejects after my appeal?
Your analysis includes a 4-level escalation plan — from insurer Grievance Officer to IRDAI Bima Bharosa portal to Insurance Ombudsman (free). We tell you exactly what to do at each level.
Why ₹999? Why not a percentage?
Flat fee means our interests are aligned from the start. We're not incentivised to only take "easy" cases or filter you out. ₹999 vs ₹5,502 average elsewhere — we think that's obvious.
